Fórmula general

Ejemplo

$x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
$$-5x^2 + 3x + 2 = 0$$
$$\begin{aligned} x &= \dfrac{-(3) \pm \sqrt{(3)^2 - 4(-5)(2)}}{2(-5)} \\ x &= \dfrac{-3 \pm \sqrt{9 + 40}}{-10} \\ x &= \dfrac{-3 \pm \sqrt{49}}{-10} \\ x &= \dfrac{-3 \pm 7}{-10} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x_1 &= \dfrac{-3 + 7}{-10} \\ x_1 &= \dfrac{4}{-10} \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 = -0.4$
$$\begin{aligned} x_2 &= \dfrac{-3 - 7}{-10} \\ x_2 &= \dfrac{-10}{-10} \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 = 1$

Respuestas

▼
Ejercicio 1
$$3x^2 - 4x - 1 = 0$$
$$\begin{aligned} x &= \dfrac{-(-4) \pm \sqrt{(-4)^2 - 4(3)(-1)}}{2(3)} \\ x &= \dfrac{4 \pm \sqrt{16 + 12}}{6} \\ x &= \dfrac{4 \pm \sqrt{28}}{6} \\ x &\approx \dfrac{4 \pm 5.3}{6} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x_1 &\approx \dfrac{4 + 5.3}{6} \\ x_1 &\approx \dfrac{9.3}{6} \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 \approx 1.55$
$$\begin{aligned} x_2 &\approx \dfrac{4 - 5.3}{6} \\ x_2 &\approx \dfrac{-1.3}{6} \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 \approx -0.21$
Ejercicio 2
$$-2x^2 + 3x - 5 = 0$$
$$\begin{aligned} x &= \dfrac{-(3) \pm \sqrt{(3)^2 - 4(-2)(-5)}}{2(-2)} \\ x &= \dfrac{-3 \pm \sqrt{9 - 40}}{-4} \\ x &= \dfrac{-3 \pm \sqrt{-31}}{-4} \end{aligned}$$

La raíz da negativo: no existe solución en $\mathbb{R}$.

Ejercicio 3
$$2x^2 + 6x - 3 = 0$$
$$\begin{aligned} x &= \dfrac{-(6) \pm \sqrt{(6)^2 - 4(2)(-3)}}{2(2)} \\ x &= \dfrac{-6 \pm \sqrt{36 + 24}}{4} \\ x &= \dfrac{-6 \pm \sqrt{60}}{4} \\ x &\approx \dfrac{-6 \pm 7.7}{4} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x_1 &\approx \dfrac{-6 + 7.7}{4} \\ x_1 &\approx \dfrac{1.7}{4} \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 \approx 0.42$
$$\begin{aligned} x_2 &\approx \dfrac{-6 - 7.7}{4} \\ x_2 &\approx \dfrac{-13.7}{4} \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 \approx -3.42$
Ejercicio 4
$$-5x^2 + 4x + 2 = 0$$
$$\begin{aligned} x &= \dfrac{-(4) \pm \sqrt{(4)^2 - 4(-5)(2)}}{2(-5)} \\ x &= \dfrac{-4 \pm \sqrt{16 + 40}}{-10} \\ x &= \dfrac{-4 \pm \sqrt{56}}{-10} \\ x &\approx \dfrac{-4 \pm 7.5}{-10} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x_1 &\approx \dfrac{-4 + 7.5}{-10} \\ x_1 &\approx \dfrac{3.5}{-10} \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 \approx -0.35$
$$\begin{aligned} x_2 &\approx \dfrac{-4 - 7.5}{-10} \\ x_2 &\approx \dfrac{-11.5}{-10} \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 \approx 1.15$
Ejercicio 5
$$3x^2 - 2x - 1 = 0$$
$$\begin{aligned} x &= \dfrac{-(-2) \pm \sqrt{(-2)^2 - 4(3)(-1)}}{2(3)} \\ x &= \dfrac{2 \pm \sqrt{4 + 12}}{6} \\ x &= \dfrac{2 \pm \sqrt{16}}{6} \\ x &= \dfrac{2 \pm 4}{6} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x_1 &= \dfrac{2 + 4}{6} \\ x_1 &= \dfrac{6}{6} \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 = 1$
$$\begin{aligned} x_2 &= \dfrac{2 - 4}{6} \\ x_2 &= \dfrac{-2}{6} \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 \approx -0.3$
