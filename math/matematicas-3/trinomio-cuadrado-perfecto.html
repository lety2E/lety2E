Completar el trinomio cuadrado perfecto

Video

Ejemplo

$$2x^2 - 12x + 10 = 0$$
$$\begin{aligned} \dfrac{2x^2 - 12x + 10}{2} &= \dfrac{0}{2} \\ x^2 - 6x + 5 &= 0 \\ x^2 - 6x &= -5 \end{aligned}$$
b$-6$
mitad$-3$
cuadrado de la mitad$9$
$$\begin{aligned} x^2 - 6x + 9 &= -5 + 9 \\ (x - 3)(x - 3) &= 4 \\ (x - 3)^2 &= 4 \\ \sqrt{(x-3)^2} &= \sqrt{4} \\ x - 3 &= \pm 2 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x - 3 &= +2 \\ x &= 2 + 3 \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 = 5$
$$\begin{aligned} x - 3 &= -2 \\ x &= -2 + 3 \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 = 1$

Respuestas

▼
Ejercicio 1
$$3x^2 - 30x + 27 = 0$$
$$\begin{aligned} \dfrac{3x^2 - 30x + 27}{3} &= \dfrac{0}{3} \\ x^2 - 10x + 9 &= 0 \\ x^2 - 10x &= -9 \end{aligned}$$
b$-10$
mitad$-5$
cuadrado de la mitad$25$
$$\begin{aligned} x^2 - 10x + 25 &= -9 + 25 \\ (x - 5)(x - 5) &= 16 \\ (x - 5)^2 &= 16 \\ \sqrt{(x-5)^2} &= \sqrt{16} \\ x - 5 &= \pm 4 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x - 5 &= +4 \\ x &= 4 + 5 \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 = 9$
$$\begin{aligned} x - 5 &= -4 \\ x &= -4 + 5 \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 = 1$
Ejercicio 2
$$3x^2 + 6x - 45 = 0$$
$$\begin{aligned} \dfrac{3x^2 + 6x - 45}{3} &= \dfrac{0}{3} \\ x^2 + 2x - 15 &= 0 \\ x^2 + 2x &= +15 \end{aligned}$$
b$2$
mitad$1$
cuadrado de la mitad$1$
$$\begin{aligned} x^2 + 2x + 1 &= 15 + 1 \\ (x + 1)(x + 1) &= 16 \\ (x + 1)^2 &= 16 \\ \sqrt{(x+1)^2} &= \sqrt{16} \\ x + 1 &= \pm 4 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x + 1 &= +4 \\ x &= 4 - 1 \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 = 3$
$$\begin{aligned} x + 1 &= -4 \\ x &= -4 - 1 \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 = -5$
Ejercicio 3
$$2x^2 - 4x - 16 = 0$$
$$\begin{aligned} \dfrac{2x^2 - 4x - 16}{2} &= \dfrac{0}{2} \\ x^2 - 2x - 8 &= 0 \\ x^2 - 2x &= +8 \end{aligned}$$
b$-2$
mitad$-1$
cuadrado de la mitad$1$
$$\begin{aligned} x^2 - 2x + 1 &= 8 + 1 \\ (x - 1)(x - 1) &= 9 \\ (x - 1)^2 &= 9 \\ \sqrt{(x-1)^2} &= \sqrt{9} \\ x - 1 &= \pm 3 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x - 1 &= +3 \\ x &= 3 + 1 \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 = 4$
$$\begin{aligned} x - 1 &= -3 \\ x &= -3 + 1 \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 = -2$
Ejercicio 4
$$2x^2 - 12x - 32 = 0$$
$$\begin{aligned} \dfrac{2x^2 - 12x - 32}{2} &= \dfrac{0}{2} \\ x^2 - 6x - 16 &= 0 \\ x^2 - 6x &= +16 \end{aligned}$$
b$-6$
mitad$-3$
cuadrado de la mitad$9$
$$\begin{aligned} x^2 - 6x + 9 &= 16 + 9 \\ (x - 3)(x - 3) &= 25 \\ (x - 3)^2 &= 25 \\ \sqrt{(x-3)^2} &= \sqrt{25} \\ x - 3 &= \pm 5 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x - 3 &= +5 \\ x &= 5 + 3 \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 = 8$
$$\begin{aligned} x - 3 &= -5 \\ x &= -5 + 3 \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 = -2$
