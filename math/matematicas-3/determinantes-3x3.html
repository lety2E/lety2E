Determinantes 3×3

Video

Ejemplo

$$\begin{cases} 3x - 2y + 4z = 2 \\ x + y - 5z = 7 \\ 2x - 5y - z = 5 \end{cases}$$
Calculamos $x$
$$x = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 2 & -2 & 4 \\ 7 & 1 & -5 \\ 5 & -5 & -1 \\ 2 & -2 & 4 \\ 7 & 1 & -5 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 3 & -2 & 4 \\ 1 & 1 & -5 \\ 2 & -5 & -1 \\ 3 & -2 & 4 \\ 1 & 1 & -5 \end{vmatrix}}$$
$$= \dfrac{-2 - 140 + 50 - (14 + 50 + 20)}{-3 - 20 + 20 - (2 + 75 + 8)} = \dfrac{-92 - 84}{-3 - 85} = \dfrac{-176}{-88}$$
$x = 2$
Calculamos $y$
$$y = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 3 & 2 & 4 \\ 1 & 7 & -5 \\ 2 & 5 & -1 \\ 3 & 2 & 4 \\ 1 & 7 & -5 \end{vmatrix}}{-88}$$
$$= \dfrac{-21 + 20 - 20 - (-2 - 75 + 56)}{-88} = \dfrac{-21 + 21}{-88} = \dfrac{0}{-88}$$
$y = 0$
Calculamos $z$
$$z = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 3 & -2 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 & 7 \\ 2 & -5 & 5 \\ 3 & -2 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 & 7 \end{vmatrix}}{-88}$$
$$= \dfrac{15 - 10 - 28 - (-10 - 105 + 4)}{-88} = \dfrac{-23 + 111}{-88} = \dfrac{88}{-88}$$
$z = -1$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 3(2) - 2(0) + 4(-1) &= 2 \\ 6 - 0 - 4 &= 2 \\ 2 &= 2 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} (2) + (0) - 5(-1) &= 7 \\ 2 + 0 + 5 &= 7 \\ 7 &= 7 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 2(2) - 5(0) - (-1) &= 5 \\ 4 - 0 + 1 &= 5 \\ 5 &= 5 \end{aligned}$$

Respuestas

▼
Ejercicio 1
$$\begin{cases} -x + 2y + z = 5 \\ -x + 3y - 2z = 11 \\ 2x - y + z = -4 \end{cases}$$
Calculamos $x$
$$x = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 5 & 2 & 1 \\ 11 & 3 & -2 \\ -4 & -1 & 1 \\ 5 & 2 & 1 \\ 11 & 3 & -2 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} -1 & 2 & 1 \\ -1 & 3 & -2 \\ 2 & -1 & 1 \\ -1 & 2 & 1 \\ -1 & 3 & -2 \end{vmatrix}}$$
$$= \dfrac{15 - 11 + 16 - (22 + 10 - 12)}{-3 + 1 - 8 - (-2 - 2 + 6)} = \dfrac{20 - 20}{-10 - 2} = \dfrac{0}{-12}$$
$x = 0$
Calculamos $y$
$$y = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} -1 & 5 & 1 \\ -1 & 11 & -2 \\ 2 & -4 & 1 \\ -1 & 5 & 1 \\ -1 & 11 & -2 \end{vmatrix}}{-12}$$
$$= \dfrac{-11 + 4 - 20 - (-5 - 8 + 22)}{-12} = \dfrac{-27 - 9}{-12} = \dfrac{-36}{-12}$$
$y = 3$
Calculamos $z$
$$z = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} -1 & 2 & 5 \\ -1 & 3 & 11 \\ 2 & -1 & -4 \\ -1 & 2 & 5 \\ -1 & 3 & 11 \end{vmatrix}}{-12}$$
$$= \dfrac{12 + 5 + 44 - (8 + 11 + 30)}{-12} = \dfrac{61 - 49}{-12} = \dfrac{12}{-12}$$
$z = -1$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} -(0) + 2(3) + (-1) &= 5 \\ -0 + 6 - 1 &= 5 \\ 5 &= 5 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} -(0) + 3(3) - 2(-1) &= 11 \\ -0 + 9 + 2 &= 11 \\ 11 &= 11 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 2(0) - (3) + (-1) &= -4 \\ 0 - 3 - 1 &= -4 \\ -4 &= -4 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 2
$$\begin{cases} 2x + 3y - z = 5 \\ x - y + z = 1 \\ 3x + y - z = 7 \end{cases}$$
Calculamos $x$
$$x = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 5 & 3 & -1 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 7 & 1 & -1 \\ 5 & 3 & -1 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 & -1 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 3 & 1 & -1 \\ 2 & 3 & -1 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \end{vmatrix}}$$
$$= \dfrac{5 - 1 + 21 - (-3 + 5 + 7)}{2 - 1 + 9 - (-3 + 2 + 3)} = \dfrac{25 - 9}{10 - 2} = \dfrac{16}{8}$$
$x = 2$
Calculamos $y$
$$y = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 5 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 3 & 7 & -1 \\ 2 & 5 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix}}{8}$$
$$= \dfrac{-2 - 7 + 15 - (-5 + 14 - 3)}{8} = \dfrac{6 - 6}{8} = \dfrac{0}{8}$$
$y = 0$
Calculamos $z$
$$z = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 & 5 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 3 & 1 & 7 \\ 2 & 3 & 5 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \end{vmatrix}}{8}$$
$$= \dfrac{-14 + 5 + 9 - (21 + 2 - 15)}{8} = \dfrac{0 - 8}{8} = \dfrac{-8}{8}$$
$z = -1$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 2(2) + 3(0) - (-1) &= 5 \\ 4 + 0 + 1 &= 5 \\ 5 &= 5 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} (2) - (0) + (-1) &= 1 \\ 2 - 0 - 1 &= 1 \\ 1 &= 1 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 3(2) + (0) - (-1) &= 7 \\ 6 + 0 + 1 &= 7 \\ 7 &= 7 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 3
$$\begin{cases} x - 2y + 3z = 5 \\ -x + y - 2z = -3 \\ 2x - 3y - z = 8 \end{cases}$$
Calculamos $x$
$$x = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 5 & -2 & 3 \\ -3 & 1 & -2 \\ 8 & -3 & -1 \\ 5 & -2 & 3 \\ -3 & 1 & -2 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 1 & -2 & 3 \\ -1 & 1 & -2 \\ 2 & -3 & -1 \\ 1 & -2 & 3 \\ -1 & 1 & -2 \end{vmatrix}}$$
$$= \dfrac{-5 + 27 + 32 - (-6 + 30 + 24)}{-1 + 9 + 8 - (-2 + 6 + 6)} = \dfrac{54 - 48}{16 - 10} = \dfrac{6}{6}$$
$x = 1$
Calculamos $y$
$$y = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 5 & 3 \\ -1 & -3 & -2 \\ 2 & 8 & -1 \\ 1 & 5 & 3 \\ -1 & -3 & -2 \end{vmatrix}}{6}$$
$$= \dfrac{3 - 24 - 20 - (5 - 16 - 18)}{6} = \dfrac{-41 + 29}{6} = \dfrac{-12}{6}$$
$y = -2$
Calculamos $z$
$$z = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 1 & -2 & 5 \\ -1 & 1 & -3 \\ 2 & -3 & 8 \\ 1 & -2 & 5 \\ -1 & 1 & -3 \end{vmatrix}}{6}$$
$$= \dfrac{8 + 15 + 12 - (16 + 9 + 10)}{6} = \dfrac{35 - 35}{6} = \dfrac{0}{6}$$
$z = 0$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (1) - 2(-2) + 3(0) &= 5 \\ 1 + 4 + 0 &= 5 \\ 5 &= 5 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} -(1) + (-2) - 2(0) &= -3 \\ -1 - 2 - 0 &= -3 \\ -3 &= -3 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 2(1) - 3(-2) - (0) &= 8 \\ 2 + 6 - 0 &= 8 \\ 8 &= 8 \end{aligned}$$
