Ejemplo

$$f(x) = x^2 - 8x + 13$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= x^2 - 8x + 16 - 16 + 13 \\ f(x) &= (x - 4)(x - 4) - 3 \\ f(x) &= (x - 4)^2 - 3 \end{aligned}$$
b$-8$
mitad$-4$
cuadrado de la mitad$16$
$V = (4,\, -3)$
$x$$f(x)$$(x)^2 - 8(x) + 13$
$2$$\mathbf{1}$$(2)^2 - 8(2) + 13 = 4 - 16 + 13 = \mathbf{1}$
$3$$\mathbf{-2}$$(3)^2 - 8(3) + 13 = 9 - 24 + 13 = \mathbf{-2}$
$4$$\mathbf{-3}$$(4)^2 - 8(4) + 13 = 16 - 32 + 13 = \mathbf{-3}$
$5$$\mathbf{-2}$$(5)^2 - 8(5) + 13 = 25 - 40 + 13 = \mathbf{-2}$
$6$$\mathbf{1}$$(6)^2 - 8(6) + 13 = 36 - 48 + 13 = \mathbf{1}$
