Ejemplo

A partir de la ecuación general se encuentran el centro y el radio completando los trinomios cuadrados perfectos.

$$x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 4y - 20 = 0$$
b$2$
mitad$1$
mitad²$1$
b$-4$
mitad$-2$
mitad²$4$
$$\begin{aligned} x^2 + 2x + y^2 - 4y &= 20 \\ x^2 + 2x + 1 + y^2 - 4y + 4 &= 20 + 1 + 4 \\ (x + 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 &= 25 \\ (x + 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 &= (5)^2 \\ (x - (-1))^2 + (y - (2))^2 &= (5)^2 \end{aligned}$$
$C = (-1, 2)$
$r = 5$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (-6)^2 + (2)^2 + 2(-6) - 4(2) - 20 &= 0 \\ 36 + 4 - 12 - 8 - 20 &= 0 \\ 40 - 40 &= 0 \\ 0 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$

Respuestas

▼
Ejercicio 1
$$x^2 + y^2 - 8x + 6y + 16 = 0$$
b$-8$
mitad$-4$
mitad²$16$
b$6$
mitad$3$
mitad²$9$
$$\begin{aligned} x^2 - 8x + y^2 + 6y &= -16 \\ x^2 - 8x + 16 + y^2 + 6y + 9 &= -16 + 16 + 9 \\ (x - 4)^2 + (y + 3)^2 &= 9 \\ (x - 4)^2 + (y + 3)^2 &= (3)^2 \\ (x - (4))^2 + (y - (-3))^2 &= (3)^2 \end{aligned}$$
$C = (4, -3)$
$r = 3$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (4)^2 + (0)^2 - 8(4) + 6(0) + 16 &= 0 \\ 16 + 0 - 32 + 0 + 16 &= 0 \\ 32 - 32 &= 0 \\ 0 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 2
$$x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 10y + 33 = 0$$
b$-6$
mitad$-3$
mitad²$9$
b$10$
mitad$5$
mitad²$25$
$$\begin{aligned} x^2 - 6x + y^2 + 10y &= -33 \\ x^2 - 6x + 9 + y^2 + 10y + 25 &= -33 + 9 + 25 \\ (x - 3)^2 + (y + 5)^2 &= 1 \\ (x - 3)^2 + (y + 5)^2 &= (1)^2 \\ (x - (3))^2 + (y - (-5))^2 &= (1)^2 \end{aligned}$$
$C = (3, -5)$
$r = 1$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (4)^2 + (-5)^2 - 6(4) + 10(-5) + 33 &= 0 \\ 16 + 25 - 24 - 50 + 33 &= 0 \\ 74 - 74 &= 0 \\ 0 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 3
$$x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 10y + 17 = 0$$
b$2$
mitad$1$
mitad²$1$
b$-10$
mitad$-5$
mitad²$25$
$$\begin{aligned} x^2 + 2x + y^2 - 10y &= -17 \\ x^2 + 2x + 1 + y^2 - 10y + 25 &= -17 + 1 + 25 \\ (x + 1)^2 + (y - 5)^2 &= 9 \\ (x + 1)^2 + (y - 5)^2 &= (3)^2 \\ (x - (-1))^2 + (y - (5))^2 &= (3)^2 \end{aligned}$$
$C = (-1, 5)$
$r = 3$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (-1)^2 + (2)^2 + 2(-1) - 10(2) + 17 &= 0 \\ 1 + 4 - 2 - 20 + 17 &= 0 \\ 22 - 22 &= 0 \\ 0 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 4
$$x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 6y - 12 = 0$$
b$-4$
mitad$-2$
mitad²$4$
b$-6$
mitad$-3$
mitad²$9$
$$\begin{aligned} x^2 - 4x + y^2 - 6y &= 12 \\ x^2 - 4x + 4 + y^2 - 6y + 9 &= 12 + 4 + 9 \\ (x - 2)^2 + (y - 3)^2 &= 25 \\ (x - 2)^2 + (y - 3)^2 &= (5)^2 \\ (x - (2))^2 + (y - (3))^2 &= (5)^2 \end{aligned}$$
$C = (2, 3)$
$r = 5$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (2)^2 + (8)^2 - 4(2) - 6(8) - 12 &= 0 \\ 4 + 64 - 8 - 48 - 12 &= 0 \\ 68 - 68 &= 0 \\ 0 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
