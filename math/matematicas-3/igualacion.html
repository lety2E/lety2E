Método de igualación

Ejemplo

$$\begin{cases} 2x - 3y = 12 \\ 2x - 8y = 22 \end{cases}$$
Despejamos e igualamos
$$\begin{aligned} 2x &= 12 + 3y \\ 2x &= 22 + 8y \end{aligned}$$ $12 + 3y = 22 + 8y$ $$\begin{aligned} 3y - 8y &= 22 - 12 \\ -5y &= 10 \\ y &= \dfrac{10}{-5} \end{aligned}$$ $y = -2$
Sustituimos
$$\begin{aligned} 2x - 3(-2) &= 12 \\ 2x + 6 &= 12 \\ 2x &= 12 - 6 \\ 2x &= 6 \\ x &= \dfrac{6}{2} \end{aligned}$$ $x = 3$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 2(3) - 3(-2) &= 12 \\ 6 + 6 &= 12 \\ 12 &= 12 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 2(3) - 8(-2) &= 22 \\ 6 + 16 &= 22 \\ 22 &= 22 \end{aligned}$$

Respuestas

▼
Ejercicio 1
$$\begin{cases} 3x - 2y = 9 \\ 3x + 4y = -9 \end{cases}$$
Despejamos e igualamos
$$\begin{aligned} 3x &= 9 + 2y \\ 3x &= -9 - 4y \end{aligned}$$ $-9 - 4y = 9 + 2y$ $$\begin{aligned} -4y - 2y &= 9 + 9 \\ -6y &= 18 \\ y &= \dfrac{18}{-6} \end{aligned}$$ $y = -3$
Sustituimos
$$\begin{aligned} 3x - 2(-3) &= 9 \\ 3x + 6 &= 9 \\ 3x &= 9 - 6 \\ 3x &= 3 \\ x &= \dfrac{3}{3} \end{aligned}$$ $x = 1$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 3(1) - 2(-3) &= 9 \\ 3 + 6 &= 9 \\ 9 &= 9 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 3(1) + 4(-3) &= -9 \\ 3 - 12 &= -9 \\ -9 &= -9 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 2
$$\begin{cases} 6x - 2y = 2 \\ 6x + 3y = 12 \end{cases}$$
Despejamos e igualamos
$$\begin{aligned} 6x &= 2 + 2y \\ 6x &= 12 - 3y \end{aligned}$$ $12 - 3y = 2 + 2y$ $$\begin{aligned} -3y - 2y &= 2 - 12 \\ -5y &= -10 \\ y &= \dfrac{-10}{-5} \end{aligned}$$ $y = 2$
Sustituimos
$$\begin{aligned} 6x - 2(2) &= 2 \\ 6x - 4 &= 2 \\ 6x &= 2 + 4 \\ 6x &= 6 \\ x &= \dfrac{6}{6} \end{aligned}$$ $x = 1$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 6(1) - 2(2) &= 2 \\ 6 - 4 &= 2 \\ 2 &= 2 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 6(1) + 3(2) &= 12 \\ 6 + 6 &= 12 \\ 12 &= 12 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 3
$$\begin{cases} x + 2y = 4 \\ 4x + 2y = -2 \end{cases}$$
Despejamos e igualamos
$$\begin{aligned} 2y &= 4 - x \\ 2y &= -2 - 4x \end{aligned}$$ $-2 - 4x = 4 - x$ $$\begin{aligned} -4x + x &= 4 + 2 \\ -3x &= 6 \\ x &= \dfrac{6}{-3} \end{aligned}$$ $x = -2$
Sustituimos
$$\begin{aligned} -2 + 2y &= 4 \\ 2y &= 4 + 2 \\ 2y &= 6 \\ y &= \dfrac{6}{2} \end{aligned}$$ $y = 3$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (-2) + 2(3) &= 4 \\ -2 + 6 &= 4 \\ 4 &= 4 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 4(-2) + 2(3) &= -2 \\ -8 + 6 &= -2 \\ -2 &= -2 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 4
$$\begin{cases} -2x + 3y = -8 \\ x + 3y = -5 \end{cases}$$
Despejamos e igualamos
$$\begin{aligned} 3y &= -8 + 2x \\ 3y &= -5 - x \end{aligned}$$ $-5 - x = -8 + 2x$ $$\begin{aligned} -x - 2x &= -8 + 5 \\ -3x &= -3 \\ x &= \dfrac{-3}{-3} \end{aligned}$$ $x = 1$
Sustituimos
$$\begin{aligned} -2(1) + 3y &= -8 \\ -2 + 3y &= -8 \\ 3y &= -8 + 2 \\ 3y &= -6 \\ y &= \dfrac{-6}{3} \end{aligned}$$ $y = -2$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} -2(1) + 3(-2) &= -8 \\ -2 - 6 &= -8 \\ -8 &= -8 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} (1) + 3(-2) &= -5 \\ 1 - 6 &= -5 \\ -5 &= -5 \end{aligned}$$
