Método de sumas y restas

Video

Ejemplo

$$\begin{array}{ccc} \begin{array}{r} \color{#7B2CBF}{(-5)} \\ \color{#7B2CBF}{(2)} \end{array} & \left\{\begin{array}{l} 2x + 4y = -8 \\ 5x - 3y = 19 \end{array}\right\} & \begin{array}{l} \color{#FF00AA}{(3)} \\ \color{#FF00AA}{(4)} \end{array} \end{array}$$
Eliminamos $x$
$$\begin{array}{r} -10x - 20y = 40 \\ 10x - 6y = 38 \\ \hline -26y = 78 \end{array}$$ $$y = \dfrac{78}{-26}$$ $y = -3$
Eliminamos $y$
$$\begin{array}{r} 6x + 12y = -24 \\ 20x - 12y = 76 \\ \hline 26x = 52 \end{array}$$ $$x = \dfrac{52}{26}$$ $x = 2$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 2(2) + 4(-3) &= -8 \\ 4 - 12 &= -8 \\ -8 &= -8 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 5(2) - 3(-3) &= 19 \\ 10 + 9 &= 19 \\ 19 &= 19 \end{aligned}$$

Respuestas

▼
Ejercicio 1
$$\begin{array}{ccc} \begin{array}{r} \color{#7B2CBF}{(-3)} \\ \color{#7B2CBF}{(2)} \end{array} & \left\{\begin{array}{l} 2x + 5y = -3 \\ 3x - 4y = 30 \end{array}\right\} & \begin{array}{l} \color{#FF00AA}{(4)} \\ \color{#FF00AA}{(5)} \end{array} \end{array}$$
Eliminamos $x$
$$\begin{array}{r} -6x - 15y = 9 \\ 6x - 8y = 60 \\ \hline -23y = 69 \end{array}$$ $$y = \dfrac{69}{-23}$$ $y = -3$
Eliminamos $y$
$$\begin{array}{r} 8x + 20y = -12 \\ 15x - 20y = 150 \\ \hline 23x = 138 \end{array}$$ $$x = \dfrac{138}{23}$$ $x = 6$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 2(6) + 5(-3) &= -3 \\ 12 - 15 &= -3 \\ -3 &= -3 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 3(6) - 4(-3) &= 30 \\ 18 + 12 &= 30 \\ 30 &= 30 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 2
$$\begin{array}{ccc} \begin{array}{r} \color{#7B2CBF}{(-2)} \\ \color{#7B2CBF}{(3)} \end{array} & \left\{\begin{array}{l} 3x - 4y = -32 \\ 2x + 2y = 2 \end{array}\right\} & \begin{array}{l} \color{#FF00AA}{(2)} \\ \color{#FF00AA}{(4)} \end{array} \end{array}$$
Eliminamos $x$
$$\begin{array}{r} -6x + 8y = 64 \\ 6x + 6y = 6 \\ \hline 14y = 70 \end{array}$$ $$y = \dfrac{70}{14}$$ $y = 5$
Eliminamos $y$
$$\begin{array}{r} 6x - 8y = -64 \\ 8x + 8y = 8 \\ \hline 14x = -56 \end{array}$$ $$x = \dfrac{-56}{14}$$ $x = -4$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 3(-4) - 4(5) &= -32 \\ -12 - 20 &= -32 \\ -32 &= -32 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 2(-4) + 2(5) &= 2 \\ -8 + 10 &= 2 \\ 2 &= 2 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 3
$$\begin{array}{ccc} \begin{array}{r} \color{#7B2CBF}{(-7)} \\ \color{#7B2CBF}{(3)} \end{array} & \left\{\begin{array}{l} 3x + 4y = 1 \\ 7x - 6y = 33 \end{array}\right\} & \begin{array}{l} \color{#FF00AA}{(6)} \\ \color{#FF00AA}{(4)} \end{array} \end{array}$$
Eliminamos $x$
$$\begin{array}{r} -21x - 28y = -7 \\ 21x - 18y = 99 \\ \hline -46y = 92 \end{array}$$ $$y = \dfrac{92}{-46}$$ $y = -2$
Eliminamos $y$
$$\begin{array}{r} 18x + 24y = 6 \\ 28x - 24y = 132 \\ \hline 46x = 138 \end{array}$$ $$x = \dfrac{138}{46}$$ $x = 3$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 3(3) + 4(-2) &= 1 \\ 9 - 8 &= 1 \\ 1 &= 1 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 7(3) - 6(-2) &= 33 \\ 21 + 12 &= 33 \\ 33 &= 33 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 4
$$\begin{array}{ccc} \begin{array}{r} \color{#7B2CBF}{(-1)} \\ \color{#7B2CBF}{(4)} \end{array} & \left\{\begin{array}{l} 4x - 2y = 22 \\ x - 8y = 13 \end{array}\right\} & \begin{array}{l} \color{#FF00AA}{(-8)} \\ \color{#FF00AA}{(2)} \end{array} \end{array}$$
Eliminamos $x$
$$\begin{array}{r} -4x + 2y = -22 \\ 4x - 32y = 52 \\ \hline -30y = 30 \end{array}$$ $$y = \dfrac{30}{-30}$$ $y = -1$
Eliminamos $y$
$$\begin{array}{r} -32x + 16y = -176 \\ 2x - 16y = 26 \\ \hline -30x = -150 \end{array}$$ $$x = \dfrac{-150}{-30}$$ $x = 5$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 4(5) - 2(-1) &= 22 \\ 20 + 2 &= 22 \\ 22 &= 22 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} (5) - 8(-1) &= 13 \\ 5 + 8 &= 13 \\ 13 &= 13 \end{aligned}$$
