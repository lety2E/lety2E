Método de sustitución

Video

Ejemplo

$$\begin{cases} 3x + y = 10 \\ 5x - 2y = 2 \end{cases} \longrightarrow \color{#7B2CBF}{y = 10 - 3x}$$
Sustituimos
$$\begin{aligned} 5x - 2(10 - 3x) &= 2 \\ 5x - 20 + 6x &= 2 \\ 11x &= 2 + 20 \\ 11x &= 22 \\ x &= \dfrac{22}{11} \end{aligned}$$ $x = 2$
Regresamos al despeje
$$\begin{aligned} y &= 10 - 3(2) \\ y &= 10 - 6 \end{aligned}$$ $y = 4$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 3(2) + (4) &= 10 \\ 6 + 4 &= 10 \\ 10 &= 10 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 5(2) - 2(4) &= 2 \\ 10 - 8 &= 2 \\ 2 &= 2 \end{aligned}$$

Respuestas

▼
Ejercicio 1
$$\begin{cases} 5x - 2y = 17 \\ x + 4y = -1 \end{cases} \longrightarrow \color{#7B2CBF}{x = -1 - 4y}$$
Sustituimos
$$\begin{aligned} 5(-1 - 4y) - 2y &= 17 \\ -5 - 20y - 2y &= 17 \\ -22y &= 17 + 5 \\ -22y &= 22 \\ y &= \dfrac{22}{-22} \end{aligned}$$ $y = -1$
Regresamos al despeje
$$\begin{aligned} x &= -1 - 4(-1) \\ x &= -1 + 4 \end{aligned}$$ $x = 3$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 5(3) - 2(-1) &= 17 \\ 15 + 2 &= 17 \\ 17 &= 17 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} (3) + 4(-1) &= -1 \\ 3 - 4 &= -1 \\ -1 &= -1 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 2
$$\begin{cases} 3x + y = -1 \\ 2x - 3y = -19 \end{cases} \longrightarrow \color{#7B2CBF}{y = -1 - 3x}$$
Sustituimos
$$\begin{aligned} 2x - 3(-1 - 3x) &= -19 \\ 2x + 3 + 9x &= -19 \\ 11x &= -19 - 3 \\ 11x &= -22 \\ x &= \dfrac{-22}{11} \end{aligned}$$ $x = -2$
Regresamos al despeje
$$\begin{aligned} y &= -1 - 3(-2) \\ y &= -1 + 6 \end{aligned}$$ $y = 5$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 3(-2) + (5) &= -1 \\ -6 + 5 &= -1 \\ -1 &= -1 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 2(-2) - 3(5) &= -19 \\ -4 - 15 &= -19 \\ -19 &= -19 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 3
$$\begin{cases} 2x + 3y = -6 \\ 5x + y = 11 \end{cases} \longrightarrow \color{#7B2CBF}{y = 11 - 5x}$$
Sustituimos
$$\begin{aligned} 2x + 3(11 - 5x) &= -6 \\ 2x + 33 - 15x &= -6 \\ -13x &= -6 - 33 \\ -13x &= -39 \\ x &= \dfrac{-39}{-13} \end{aligned}$$ $x = 3$
Regresamos al despeje
$$\begin{aligned} y &= 11 - 5(3) \\ y &= 11 - 15 \end{aligned}$$ $y = -4$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 2(3) + 3(-4) &= -6 \\ 6 - 12 &= -6 \\ -6 &= -6 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 5(3) + (-4) &= 11 \\ 15 - 4 &= 11 \\ 11 &= 11 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 4
$$\begin{cases} x + 2y = 9 \\ 3x - y = -15 \end{cases} \longrightarrow \color{#7B2CBF}{x = 9 - 2y}$$
Sustituimos
$$\begin{aligned} 3(9 - 2y) - y &= -15 \\ 27 - 6y - y &= -15 \\ -7y &= -15 - 27 \\ -7y &= -42 \\ y &= \dfrac{-42}{-7} \end{aligned}$$ $y = 6$
Regresamos al despeje
$$\begin{aligned} x &= 9 - 2(6) \\ x &= 9 - 12 \end{aligned}$$ $x = -3$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (-3) + 2(6) &= 9 \\ -3 + 12 &= 9 \\ 9 &= 9 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 3(-3) - (6) &= -15 \\ -9 - 6 &= -15 \\ -15 &= -15 \end{aligned}$$
