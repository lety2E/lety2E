Sustituimos
5x−2(10−3x)=25x−20+6x=211x=2+2011x=22x=2211\begin{aligned} 5x - 2(10 - 3x) &= 2 \\ 5x - 20 + 6x &= 2 \\ 11x &= 2 + 20 \\ 11x &= 22 \\ x &= \dfrac{22}{11} \end{aligned}5x−2(10−3x)5x−20+6x11x11xx​=2=2=2+20=22=1122​​ x=2x = 2x=2

y=10−3(2)y=10−6\begin{aligned} y &= 10 - 3(2) \\ y &= 10 - 6 \end{aligned}yy​=10−3(2)=10−6​ y=4y = 4y=4
