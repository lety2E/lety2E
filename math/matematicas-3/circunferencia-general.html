Ecuación general de la circunferencia

Video

Ejemplo

$(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$

Con el centro $C = (h, k)$ y el radio $r$ se arma la ecuación y se desarrollan los binomios.

$C = (-1, 2)$
$r = 5$
$$\begin{aligned} (x - (-1))^2 + (y - (2))^2 &= (5)^2 \\ (x + 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 &= 25 \\ x^2 + 2(x)(1) + (1)^2 + y^2 + 2(y)(-2) + (-2)^2 &= 25 \\ x^2 + 2x + 1 + y^2 - 4y + 4 &= 25 \\ x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 4y + 1 + 4 - 25 &= 0 \\ x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 4y + 5 - 25 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
$x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 4y - 20 = 0$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (-6)^2 + (2)^2 + 2(-6) - 4(2) - 20 &= 0 \\ 36 + 4 - 12 - 8 - 20 &= 0 \\ 40 - 40 &= 0 \\ 0 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$

Respuestas

▼
Ejercicio 1
$C = (-3, 4)$
$r = 5$
$$\begin{aligned} (x - (-3))^2 + (y - (4))^2 &= (5)^2 \\ (x + 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 &= 25 \\ x^2 + 2(x)(3) + (3)^2 + y^2 + 2(y)(-4) + (-4)^2 &= 25 \\ x^2 + 6x + 9 + y^2 - 8y + 16 &= 25 \\ x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 8y + 9 + 16 - 25 &= 0 \\ x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 8y + 25 - 25 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
$x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 8y = 0$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (2)^2 + (4)^2 + 6(2) - 8(4) &= 0 \\ 4 + 16 + 12 - 32 &= 0 \\ 32 - 32 &= 0 \\ 0 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 2
$C = (2, 3)$
$r = 4$
$$\begin{aligned} (x - (2))^2 + (y - (3))^2 &= (4)^2 \\ (x - 2)^2 + (y - 3)^2 &= 16 \\ x^2 + 2(x)(-2) + (-2)^2 + y^2 + 2(y)(-3) + (-3)^2 &= 16 \\ x^2 - 4x + 4 + y^2 - 6y + 9 &= 16 \\ x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 6y + 4 + 9 - 16 &= 0 \\ x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 6y + 13 - 16 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
$x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 6y - 3 = 0$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (2)^2 + (7)^2 - 4(2) - 6(7) - 3 &= 0 \\ 4 + 49 - 8 - 42 - 3 &= 0 \\ 53 - 53 &= 0 \\ 0 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 3
$C = (5, -1)$
$r = 2$
$$\begin{aligned} (x - (5))^2 + (y - (-1))^2 &= (2)^2 \\ (x - 5)^2 + (y + 1)^2 &= 4 \\ x^2 + 2(x)(-5) + (-5)^2 + y^2 + 2(y)(1) + (1)^2 &= 4 \\ x^2 - 10x + 25 + y^2 + 2y + 1 &= 4 \\ x^2 + y^2 - 10x + 2y + 25 + 1 - 4 &= 0 \\ x^2 + y^2 - 10x + 2y + 26 - 4 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
$x^2 + y^2 - 10x + 2y + 22 = 0$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (5)^2 + (-3)^2 - 10(5) + 2(-3) + 22 &= 0 \\ 25 + 9 - 50 - 6 + 22 &= 0 \\ 56 - 56 &= 0 \\ 0 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 4
$C = (-2, -1)$
$r = 3$
$$\begin{aligned} (x - (-2))^2 + (y - (-1))^2 &= (3)^2 \\ (x + 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 &= 9 \\ x^2 + 2(x)(2) + (2)^2 + y^2 + 2(y)(1) + (1)^2 &= 9 \\ x^2 + 4x + 4 + y^2 + 2y + 1 &= 9 \\ x^2 + y^2 + 4x + 2y + 4 + 1 - 9 &= 0 \\ x^2 + y^2 + 4x + 2y + 5 - 9 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
$x^2 + y^2 + 4x + 2y - 4 = 0$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (-2)^2 + (2)^2 + 4(-2) + 2(2) - 4 &= 0 \\ 4 + 4 - 8 + 4 - 4 &= 0 \\ 12 - 12 &= 0 \\ 0 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
