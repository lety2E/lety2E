Ecuaciones cuadráticas incompletas

Video

Ejemplo

Puras
$ax^2 + c = 0$ $$\begin{aligned} 4x^2 - 5 &= 7x^2 - 10 \\ 4x^2 - 7x^2 &= -10 + 5 \\ -3x^2 &= -5 \\ x^2 &= \dfrac{-5}{-3} = \dfrac{5}{3} \\ \sqrt{x^2} &= \pm\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{3}} \end{aligned}$$
$x_1 = +\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{3}}$$x_2 = -\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{3}}$
Mixtas
$ax^2 + bx = 0$ $$\begin{aligned} 7x^2 - 8x &= 6x - 3x^2 \\ 7x^2 + 3x^2 - 8x - 6x &= 0 \\ 10x^2 - 14x &= 0 \\ 2x(5x - 7) &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
$x_1 = 0$$x_2 = \dfrac{7}{5}$

Otro caso de ecuación pura

$$\begin{aligned} 2x^2 - 3 &= 5x^2 + 9 \\ 2x^2 - 5x^2 &= 9 + 3 \\ -3x^2 &= 12 \\ x^2 &= \dfrac{12}{-3} \\ x^2 &= -4 \end{aligned}$$

El cuadrado da negativo: no existe solución en $\mathbb{R}$.

Respuestas

▼

Bloque 1

$$7x^2 - 4x = 6x + 2x^2$$
$$\begin{aligned} 7x^2 - 2x^2 - 4x - 6x &= 0 \\ 5x^2 - 10x &= 0 \\ 5x(x - 2) &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 5x &= 0 \\ x &= 0 \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 = 0$
$$\begin{aligned} x - 2 &= 0 \\ x &= 2 \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 = 2$
$$4x^2 - 5 = 3x^2 - 2$$
$$\begin{aligned} 4x^2 - 3x^2 &= -2 + 5 \\ x^2 &= 3 \\ \sqrt{x^2} &= \pm\sqrt{3} \end{aligned}$$
$x_1 = +\sqrt{3}$$x_2 = -\sqrt{3}$

Bloque 2

$$8x^2 - 3x = -x + 5x^2$$
$$\begin{aligned} 8x^2 - 5x^2 - 3x + x &= 0 \\ 3x^2 - 2x &= 0 \\ x(3x - 2) &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x &= 0 \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 = 0$
$$\begin{aligned} 3x - 2 &= 0 \\ 3x &= 2 \\ x &= \dfrac{2}{3} \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 = \dfrac{2}{3}$
$$-2x^2 + 3 = 5x^2 - 8$$
$$\begin{aligned} -2x^2 - 5x^2 &= -8 - 3 \\ -7x^2 &= -11 \\ x^2 &= \dfrac{-11}{-7} = \dfrac{11}{7} \\ \sqrt{x^2} &= \pm\sqrt{\dfrac{11}{7}} \end{aligned}$$
$x_1 = +\sqrt{\dfrac{11}{7}}$$x_2 = -\sqrt{\dfrac{11}{7}}$

Bloque 3

$$3x - 4x^2 = 8x^2 - 4x$$
$$\begin{aligned} -4x^2 - 8x^2 + 3x + 4x &= 0 \\ -12x^2 + 7x &= 0 \\ x(-12x + 7) &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} x &= 0 \end{aligned}$$ $x_1 = 0$
$$\begin{aligned} -12x + 7 &= 0 \\ -12x &= -7 \\ x &= \dfrac{-7}{-12} = \dfrac{7}{12} \end{aligned}$$ $x_2 = \dfrac{7}{12}$
$$-2 + 3x^2 = 6 + 9x^2$$
$$\begin{aligned} 3x^2 - 9x^2 &= 6 + 2 \\ -6x^2 &= 8 \\ x^2 &= -\dfrac{8}{6} \end{aligned}$$

El cuadrado da negativo: no existe solución en $\mathbb{R}$.
