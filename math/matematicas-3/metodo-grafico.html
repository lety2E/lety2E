Método gráfico

Ejemplo

$$\begin{cases} x + 2y = 4 \\ 4x + 2y = -2 \end{cases}$$
Primera recta
$$\begin{aligned} x + 2y &= 4 \\ 2y &= 4 - x \\ y &= \dfrac{4-x}{2} \end{aligned}$$ $y = 2 - \dfrac{1}{2}x$ $$b = 2 \qquad m = \dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$$
Segunda recta
$$\begin{aligned} 4x + 2y &= -2 \\ 2y &= -2 - 4x \\ y &= \dfrac{-2-4x}{2} \end{aligned}$$ $y = -1 - \dfrac{4}{2}x$ $$b = -1 \qquad m = \dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = -\dfrac{4}{2}$$
$x = -2$$y = 3$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (-2) + 2(3) &= 4 \\ -2 + 6 &= 4 \\ 4 &= 4 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 4(-2) + 2(3) &= -2 \\ -8 + 6 &= -2 \\ -2 &= -2 \end{aligned}$$

Respuestas

▼
Ejercicio 1
$$\begin{cases} x + 2y = 8 \\ x - 2y = -4 \end{cases}$$
Primera recta
$$\begin{aligned} x + 2y &= 8 \\ 2y &= 8 - x \\ y &= \dfrac{8-x}{2} \end{aligned}$$ $y = 4 - \dfrac{1}{2}x$ $$b = 4 \qquad m = \dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$$
Segunda recta
$$\begin{aligned} x - 2y &= -4 \\ -2y &= -4 - x \\ y &= \dfrac{-4-x}{-2} \end{aligned}$$ $y = 2 + \dfrac{1}{2}x$ $$b = 2 \qquad m = \dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \dfrac{1}{2}$$
$x = 2$$y = 3$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (2) + 2(3) &= 8 \\ 2 + 6 &= 8 \\ 8 &= 8 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} (2) - 2(3) &= -4 \\ 2 - 6 &= -4 \\ -4 &= -4 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 2
$$\begin{cases} -x + 3y = 12 \\ 2x + 3y = 3 \end{cases}$$
Primera recta
$$\begin{aligned} -x + 3y &= 12 \\ 3y &= 12 + x \\ y &= \dfrac{12+x}{3} \end{aligned}$$ $y = 4 + \dfrac{1}{3}x$ $$b = 4 \qquad m = \dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \dfrac{1}{3}$$
Segunda recta
$$\begin{aligned} 2x + 3y &= 3 \\ 3y &= 3 - 2x \\ y &= \dfrac{3-2x}{3} \end{aligned}$$ $y = 1 - \dfrac{2}{3}x$ $$b = 1 \qquad m = \dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = -\dfrac{2}{3}$$
$x = -3$$y = 3$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} -(-3) + 3(3) &= 12 \\ 3 + 9 &= 12 \\ 12 &= 12 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 2(-3) + 3(3) &= 3 \\ -6 + 9 &= 3 \\ 3 &= 3 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 3
$$\begin{cases} -x + 3y = -6 \\ x - y = 4 \end{cases}$$
Primera recta
$$\begin{aligned} -x + 3y &= -6 \\ 3y &= -6 + x \\ y &= \dfrac{-6+x}{3} \end{aligned}$$ $y = -2 + \dfrac{1}{3}x$ $$b = -2 \qquad m = \dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \dfrac{1}{3}$$
Segunda recta
$$\begin{aligned} x - y &= 4 \\ -y &= 4 - x \\ y &= \dfrac{4-x}{-1} \end{aligned}$$ $y = -4 + \dfrac{1}{1}x$ $$b = -4 \qquad m = \dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \dfrac{1}{1}$$
$x = 3$$y = -1$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} -(3) + 3(-1) &= -6 \\ -3 - 3 &= -6 \\ -6 &= -6 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} (3) - (-1) &= 4 \\ 3 + 1 &= 4 \\ 4 &= 4 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 4
$$\begin{cases} 2x + y = 3 \\ 3x - 2y = 8 \end{cases}$$
Primera recta
$$\begin{aligned} 2x + y &= 3 \\ y &= 3 - 2x \\ y &= \dfrac{3-2x}{1} \end{aligned}$$ $y = 3 - \dfrac{2}{1}x$ $$b = 3 \qquad m = \dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = -\dfrac{2}{1}$$
Segunda recta
$$\begin{aligned} 3x - 2y &= 8 \\ -2y &= 8 - 3x \\ y &= \dfrac{8-3x}{-2} \end{aligned}$$ $y = -4 + \dfrac{3}{2}x$ $$b = -4 \qquad m = \dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \dfrac{3}{2}$$
$x = 2$$y = -1$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 2(2) + (-1) &= 3 \\ 4 - 1 &= 3 \\ 3 &= 3 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 3(2) - 2(-1) &= 8 \\ 6 + 2 &= 8 \\ 8 &= 8 \end{aligned}$$
