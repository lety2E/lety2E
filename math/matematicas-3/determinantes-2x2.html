Determinantes 2×2

Video

Ejemplo

$$\begin{cases} 5x - 3y = 21 \\ 2x + y = 4 \end{cases}$$
Calculamos $x$
$$x = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 21 & -3 \\ 4 & 1 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 5 & -3 \\ 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix}} = \dfrac{21 + 12}{5 + 6} = \dfrac{33}{11}$$ $x = 3$
Calculamos $y$
$$y = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 5 & 21 \\ 2 & 4 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 5 & -3 \\ 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix}} = \dfrac{20 - 42}{5 + 6} = \dfrac{-22}{11}$$ $y = -2$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 5(3) - 3(-2) &= 21 \\ 15 + 6 &= 21 \\ 21 &= 21 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 2(3) + (-2) &= 4 \\ 6 - 2 &= 4 \\ 4 &= 4 \end{aligned}$$

Respuestas

▼
Ejercicio 1
$$\begin{cases} 2x + 4y = -10 \\ 3x - 2y = -3 \end{cases}$$
Calculamos $x$
$$x = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} -10 & 4 \\ -3 & -2 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 4 \\ 3 & -2 \end{vmatrix}} = \dfrac{20 + 12}{-4 - 12} = \dfrac{32}{-16}$$ $x = -2$
Calculamos $y$
$$y = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 2 & -10 \\ 3 & -3 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 4 \\ 3 & -2 \end{vmatrix}} = \dfrac{-6 + 30}{-4 - 12} = \dfrac{24}{-16}$$ $y = -1.5$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 2(-2) + 4(-1.5) &= -10 \\ -4 - 6 &= -10 \\ -10 &= -10 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 3(-2) - 2(-1.5) &= -3 \\ -6 + 3 &= -3 \\ -3 &= -3 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 2
$$\begin{cases} 5x - 2y = 4 \\ 3x - 4y = -6 \end{cases}$$
Calculamos $x$
$$x = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 4 & -2 \\ -6 & -4 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 5 & -2 \\ 3 & -4 \end{vmatrix}} = \dfrac{-16 - 12}{-20 + 6} = \dfrac{-28}{-14}$$ $x = 2$
Calculamos $y$
$$y = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 5 & 4 \\ 3 & -6 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 5 & -2 \\ 3 & -4 \end{vmatrix}} = \dfrac{-30 - 12}{-20 + 6} = \dfrac{-42}{-14}$$ $y = 3$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 5(2) - 2(3) &= 4 \\ 10 - 6 &= 4 \\ 4 &= 4 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 3(2) - 4(3) &= -6 \\ 6 - 12 &= -6 \\ -6 &= -6 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 3
$$\begin{cases} x + 3y = 1 \\ 2x - 5y = -9 \end{cases}$$
Calculamos $x$
$$x = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ -9 & -5 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 2 & -5 \end{vmatrix}} = \dfrac{-5 + 27}{-5 - 6} = \dfrac{22}{-11}$$ $x = -2$
Calculamos $y$
$$y = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 2 & -9 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 2 & -5 \end{vmatrix}} = \dfrac{-9 - 2}{-5 - 6} = \dfrac{-11}{-11}$$ $y = 1$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} (-2) + 3(1) &= 1 \\ -2 + 3 &= 1 \\ 1 &= 1 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} 2(-2) - 5(1) &= -9 \\ -4 - 5 &= -9 \\ -9 &= -9 \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 4
$$\begin{cases} 2x + 3y = -2 \\ -2x - 6y = -4 \end{cases}$$
Calculamos $x$
$$x = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} -2 & 3 \\ -4 & -6 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ -2 & -6 \end{vmatrix}} = \dfrac{12 + 12}{-12 + 6} = \dfrac{24}{-6}$$ $x = -4$
Calculamos $y$
$$y = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} 2 & -2 \\ -2 & -4 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ -2 & -6 \end{vmatrix}} = \dfrac{-8 - 4}{-12 + 6} = \dfrac{-12}{-6}$$ $y = 2$

Comprobación

$$\begin{aligned} 2(-4) + 3(2) &= -2 \\ -8 + 6 &= -2 \\ -2 &= -2 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} -2(-4) - 6(2) &= -4 \\ 8 - 12 &= -4 \\ -4 &= -4 \end{aligned}$$
