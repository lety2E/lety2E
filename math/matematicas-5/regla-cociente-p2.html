Regla del cociente (P2)

Apuntes

La misma regla del cociente, ahora con senos, cosenos y tangentes:

$$f(x) = \frac{u}{v} \implies f'(x) = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}$$

Ejemplos

$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{3x^2}{5\cos x} \\ f'(x) &= \frac{(6x)(5\cos x) - (3x^2)(-5\operatorname{sen} x)}{(5\cos x)^2} \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{30x\cos x + 15x^2\operatorname{sen} x}{25\cos^2 x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{-6x^2 + 3\cos x}{\operatorname{sen} x} \\ f'(x) &= \frac{(-12x - 3\operatorname{sen} x)(\operatorname{sen} x) - (-6x^2 + 3\cos x)(\cos x)}{\operatorname{sen}^2 x} \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{-12x\operatorname{sen} x - 3\operatorname{sen}^2 x + 6x^2\cos x - 3\cos^2 x}{\operatorname{sen}^2 x}} \end{aligned}$$

Ejercicios

Bloque 1
$f(x) = \dfrac{4x^2}{5\cos x}$
$g(x) = \dfrac{7x^3}{6\operatorname{sen} x}$
$h(x) = \dfrac{2\tan x}{8x^2}$
$f(x) = \dfrac{-4\cos x}{6x^2}$
Bloque 2
$f(x) = \dfrac{5x^2 - 3\operatorname{sen} x}{2\cos x}$
$g(x) = \dfrac{7\cos x}{2x^2 - 4\operatorname{sen} x}$
$h(x) = \dfrac{3x^2 - 2\operatorname{sen} x}{4\cos x}$
$f(x) = \dfrac{4x^3 + 2\cos x}{\operatorname{sen} x}$
Bloque 3
$f(x) = \dfrac{2x^4}{3\cos x}$
$g(x) = \dfrac{-5\tan x}{2x^3}$
$h(x) = \dfrac{5x^3}{2\cos x}$
$f(x) = \dfrac{2\cos x}{3x^2 - 5\operatorname{sen} x}$
$g(x) = \dfrac{4x^2 - 2\cos x}{\operatorname{sen} x}$
$h(x) = \dfrac{6\cos x}{3x^2 - 2\operatorname{sen} x}$

Respuestas

▼
Bloque 1
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{4x^2}{5\cos x} \\ f'(x) &= \frac{(8x)(5\cos x) - (4x^2)(-5\operatorname{sen} x)}{(5\cos x)^2} \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{40x\cos x + 20x^2\operatorname{sen} x}{25\cos^2 x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} g(x) &= \frac{7x^3}{6\operatorname{sen} x} \\ g'(x) &= \frac{(21x^2)(6\operatorname{sen} x) - (7x^3)(6\cos x)}{(6\operatorname{sen} x)^2} \\ \mathbf{g'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{126x^2\operatorname{sen} x - 42x^3\cos x}{36\operatorname{sen}^2 x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} h(x) &= \frac{2\tan x}{8x^2} \\ h'(x) &= \frac{(2\sec^2 x)(8x^2) - (2\tan x)(16x)}{(8x^2)^2} \\ \mathbf{h'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{16x^2\sec^2 x - 32x\tan x}{64x^4}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{-4\cos x}{6x^2} \\ f'(x) &= \frac{(4\operatorname{sen} x)(6x^2) - (-4\cos x)(12x)}{(6x^2)^2} \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{24x^2\operatorname{sen} x + 48x\cos x}{36x^4}} \end{aligned}$$
Bloque 2
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{5x^2 - 3\operatorname{sen} x}{2\cos x} \\ f'(x) &= \frac{(10x - 3\cos x)(2\cos x) - (5x^2 - 3\operatorname{sen} x)(-2\operatorname{sen} x)}{(2\cos x)^2} \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{20x\cos x - 6\cos^2 x + 10x^2\operatorname{sen} x - 6\operatorname{sen}^2 x}{4\cos^2 x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} g(x) &= \frac{7\cos x}{2x^2 - 4\operatorname{sen} x} \\ g'(x) &= \frac{(-7\operatorname{sen} x)(2x^2 - 4\operatorname{sen} x) - (7\cos x)(4x - 4\cos x)}{(2x^2 - 4\operatorname{sen} x)^2} \\ \mathbf{g'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{-14x^2\operatorname{sen} x + 28\operatorname{sen}^2 x - 28x\cos x + 28\cos^2 x}{(2x^2 - 4\operatorname{sen} x)^2}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} h(x) &= \frac{3x^2 - 2\operatorname{sen} x}{4\cos x} \\ h'(x) &= \frac{(6x - 2\cos x)(4\cos x) - (3x^2 - 2\operatorname{sen} x)(-4\operatorname{sen} x)}{(4\cos x)^2} \\ \mathbf{h'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{24x\cos x - 8\cos^2 x + 12x^2\operatorname{sen} x - 8\operatorname{sen}^2 x}{16\cos^2 x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{4x^3 + 2\cos x}{\operatorname{sen} x} \\ f'(x) &= \frac{(12x^2 - 2\operatorname{sen} x)(\operatorname{sen} x) - (4x^3 + 2\cos x)(\cos x)}{\operatorname{sen}^2 x} \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{12x^2\operatorname{sen} x - 2\operatorname{sen}^2 x - 4x^3\cos x - 2\cos^2 x}{\operatorname{sen}^2 x}} \end{aligned}$$
Bloque 3
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{2x^4}{3\cos x} \\ f'(x) &= \frac{(8x^3)(3\cos x) - (2x^4)(-3\operatorname{sen} x)}{(3\cos x)^2} \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{24x^3\cos x + 6x^4\operatorname{sen} x}{9\cos^2 x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} g(x) &= \frac{-5\tan x}{2x^3} \\ g'(x) &= \frac{(-5\sec^2 x)(2x^3) - (-5\tan x)(6x^2)}{(2x^3)^2} \\ \mathbf{g'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{-10x^3\sec^2 x + 30x^2\tan x}{4x^6}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} h(x) &= \frac{5x^3}{2\cos x} \\ h'(x) &= \frac{(15x^2)(2\cos x) - (5x^3)(-2\operatorname{sen} x)}{(2\cos x)^2} \\ \mathbf{h'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{30x^2\cos x + 10x^3\operatorname{sen} x}{4\cos^2 x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{2\cos x}{3x^2 - 5\operatorname{sen} x} \\ f'(x) &= \frac{(-2\operatorname{sen} x)(3x^2 - 5\operatorname{sen} x) - (2\cos x)(6x - 5\cos x)}{(3x^2 - 5\operatorname{sen} x)^2} \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{-6x^2\operatorname{sen} x + 10\operatorname{sen}^2 x - 12x\cos x + 10\cos^2 x}{(3x^2 - 5\operatorname{sen} x)^2}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} g(x) &= \frac{4x^2 - 2\cos x}{\operatorname{sen} x} \\ g'(x) &= \frac{(8x + 2\operatorname{sen} x)(\operatorname{sen} x) - (4x^2 - 2\cos x)(\cos x)}{\operatorname{sen}^2 x} \\ \mathbf{g'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{8x\operatorname{sen} x + 2\operatorname{sen}^2 x - 4x^2\cos x + 2\cos^2 x}{\operatorname{sen}^2 x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} h(x) &= \frac{6\cos x}{3x^2 - 2\operatorname{sen} x} \\ h'(x) &= \frac{(-6\operatorname{sen} x)(3x^2 - 2\operatorname{sen} x) - (6\cos x)(6x - 2\cos x)}{(3x^2 - 2\operatorname{sen} x)^2} \\ \mathbf{h'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{-18x^2\operatorname{sen} x + 12\operatorname{sen}^2 x - 36x\cos x + 12\cos^2 x}{(3x^2 - 2\operatorname{sen} x)^2}} \end{aligned}$$
