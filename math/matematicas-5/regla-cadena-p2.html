Regla de la cadena (P2)

Apuntes

La misma regla de la cadena, ahora con senos, cosenos, logaritmos y raíces:

$$h(x) = f(g(x)) \longrightarrow h'(x) = [f'(g(x))][g'(x)]$$

Ejemplos

$$\begin{aligned} g &= \sqrt[5]{\cos^3 x} \\ g &= (\cos x)^{3/5} \\ g' &= \frac{3}{5}(\cos x)^{\frac{3}{5}-\frac{5}{5}}(-\operatorname{sen} x) \\ g' &= \frac{3}{5}(\cos x)^{-\frac{2}{5}}(-\operatorname{sen} x) \\ g' &= -\frac{3\operatorname{sen} x}{5(\cos x)^{2/5}} \\ \mathbf{g'} &= \mathbf{-\frac{3\operatorname{sen} x}{5\sqrt[5]{\cos^2 x}}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} h &= \cos(3x - 5x^2) \\ h' &= -\operatorname{sen}(3x - 5x^2)[3 - 10x] \\ \mathbf{h'} &= \mathbf{-3\operatorname{sen}(3x - 5x^2) + 10x\operatorname{sen}(3x - 5x^2)} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} g(x) &= \ln(5x^2 - 5) \\ g'(x) &= \left[\frac{1}{5x^2 - 5}\right](10x) \\ \mathbf{g'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{10x}{5x^2 - 5}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f &= 5\operatorname{sen}^3 x \\ f &= 5(\operatorname{sen} x)^3 \\ \mathbf{f'} &= \mathbf{15\operatorname{sen}^2 x \cdot \cos x} \end{aligned}$$

Ejercicios

Bloque 1
$f = \sqrt[3]{\cos^7 x}$
$f = \cos(7x - 5x^2)$
$f = \ln(3x^2 - 4)$
$f = -5\operatorname{sen}^5 x$
Bloque 2
$f = \sqrt{\operatorname{sen}^3 x}$
$f = \operatorname{sen}(6x - 4x^2)$
$f = \ln(2x^2 - 5x)$
$f = 4\cos^3 x$

Respuestas

▼
Bloque 1
$$\begin{aligned} f &= \sqrt[3]{\cos^7 x} \\ f &= (\cos x)^{7/3} \\ f' &= \frac{7}{3}(\cos x)^{\frac{7}{3} - \frac{3}{3}}(-\operatorname{sen} x) \\ f' &= \frac{7}{3}(\cos x)^{4/3}(-\operatorname{sen} x) \\ \mathbf{f'} &= \mathbf{-\frac{7\operatorname{sen} x \cdot \sqrt[3]{\cos^4 x}}{3}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f &= \cos(7x - 5x^2) \\ f' &= -\operatorname{sen}(7x - 5x^2)[7 - 10x] \\ \mathbf{f'} &= \mathbf{-7\operatorname{sen}(7x - 5x^2) + 10x\operatorname{sen}(7x - 5x^2)} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f &= \ln(3x^2 - 4) \\ f' &= \left[\frac{1}{3x^2 - 4}\right](6x) \\ \mathbf{f'} &= \mathbf{\frac{6x}{3x^2 - 4}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f &= -5\operatorname{sen}^5 x \\ f &= -5(\operatorname{sen} x)^5 \\ f' &= -25(\operatorname{sen} x)^4 \cdot \cos x \\ \mathbf{f'} &= \mathbf{-25\operatorname{sen}^4 x \cdot \cos x} \end{aligned}$$
Bloque 2
$$\begin{aligned} f &= \sqrt{\operatorname{sen}^3 x} \\ f &= (\operatorname{sen} x)^{3/2} \\ f' &= \frac{3}{2}(\operatorname{sen} x)^{\frac{3}{2} - \frac{2}{2}}(\cos x) \\ f' &= \frac{3}{2}(\operatorname{sen} x)^{1/2}(\cos x) \\ \mathbf{f'} &= \mathbf{\frac{3\cos x \sqrt{\operatorname{sen} x}}{2}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f &= \operatorname{sen}(6x - 4x^2) \\ f' &= \cos(6x - 4x^2)[6 - 8x] \\ \mathbf{f'} &= \mathbf{6\cos(6x - 4x^2) - 8x\cos(6x - 4x^2)} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f &= \ln(2x^2 - 5x) \\ f' &= \left[\frac{1}{2x^2 - 5x}\right](4x - 5) \\ \mathbf{f'} &= \mathbf{\frac{4x - 5}{2x^2 - 5x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f &= 4\cos^3 x \\ f &= 4(\cos x)^3 \\ f' &= 12(\cos x)^2(-\operatorname{sen} x) \\ \mathbf{f'} &= \mathbf{-12\cos^2 x \cdot \operatorname{sen} x} \end{aligned}$$
