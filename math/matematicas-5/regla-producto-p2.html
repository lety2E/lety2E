Regla del producto (P2)

Apuntes

La misma regla del producto, ahora con trigonométricas, logaritmos, exponenciales y raíces:

$$f(x) = u \cdot v \implies f'(x) = u'v + uv'$$

Ejemplos

$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= 5x^3 \cos x \\ f'(x) &= (15x^2)(\cos x) + (5x^3)(-\operatorname{sen} x) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{15x^2 \cos x - 5x^3 \operatorname{sen} x} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= 7 \operatorname{sen} x \cos x \\ f'(x) &= (7 \cos x)(\cos x) + (7 \operatorname{sen} x)(-\operatorname{sen} x) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{7 \cos^2 x - 7 \operatorname{sen}^2 x} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \sqrt[3]{x^5} \ln x = x^{\frac{5}{3}} \ln x \\ f'(x) &= \left( \frac{5}{3} x^{\frac{5}{3} - \frac{3}{3}} \right)(\ln x) + (x^{\frac{5}{3}})\left( \frac{1}{x} \right) \\ f'(x) &= \frac{5}{3} x^{\frac{2}{3}} \ln x + \frac{x^{\frac{5}{3}}}{x} \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{5}{3} \sqrt[3]{x^2} \ln x + \frac{\sqrt[3]{x^5}}{x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \sqrt[5]{x} \, 7^x = x^{\frac{1}{5}} 7^x \\ f'(x) &= \left( \frac{1}{5} x^{\frac{1}{5} - \frac{5}{5}} \right)(7^x) + (x^{\frac{1}{5}})(7^x \ln 7) \\ f'(x) &= \frac{1}{5} x^{-\frac{4}{5}} 7^x + \sqrt[5]{x} \, 7^x \ln 7 \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{1}{5 \sqrt[5]{x^4}} 7^x + \sqrt[5]{x} \, 7^x \ln 7} \end{aligned}$$

Ejercicios

Bloque 1
$f(x) = -4x^2 \operatorname{sen} x$
$f(x) = 5x^4 \cos x$
$f(x) = 6x^2 \tan x$
$f(x) = 8 \operatorname{sen} x \tan x$
$f(x) = -4 \operatorname{sen} x \cos x$
Bloque 2
$f(x) = 6 \cos x \operatorname{sen} x$
$f(x) = \sqrt{x} \ln x$
$f(x) = \sqrt[3]{x^4} \ln x$
$f(x) = \sqrt[3]{x^4} e^x$
$f(x) = \sqrt[3]{x^5} \, 6^x$

Respuestas

▼
Bloque 1
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= -4x^2 \operatorname{sen} x \\ f'(x) &= (-8x)(\operatorname{sen} x) + (-4x^2)(\cos x) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{-8x \operatorname{sen} x - 4x^2 \cos x} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= 5x^4 \cos x \\ f'(x) &= (20x^3)(\cos x) + (5x^4)(-\operatorname{sen} x) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{20x^3 \cos x - 5x^4 \operatorname{sen} x} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= 6x^2 \tan x \\ f'(x) &= (12x)(\tan x) + (6x^2)(\sec^2 x) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{12x \tan x + 6x^2 \sec^2 x} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= 8 \operatorname{sen} x \tan x \\ f'(x) &= (8 \cos x)(\tan x) + (8 \operatorname{sen} x)(\sec^2 x) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{8 \cos x \tan x + 8 \operatorname{sen} x \sec^2 x} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= -4 \operatorname{sen} x \cos x \\ f'(x) &= (-4 \cos x)(\cos x) + (-4 \operatorname{sen} x)(-\operatorname{sen} x) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{-4 \cos^2 x + 4 \operatorname{sen}^2 x} \end{aligned}$$
Bloque 2
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= 6 \cos x \operatorname{sen} x \\ f'(x) &= (-6 \operatorname{sen} x)(\operatorname{sen} x) + (6 \cos x)(\cos x) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{-6 \operatorname{sen}^2 x + 6 \cos^2 x} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \sqrt{x} \ln x = x^{\frac{1}{2}} \ln x \\ f'(x) &= \left( \frac{1}{2} x^{-\frac{1}{2}} \right)(\ln x) + (x^{\frac{1}{2}})\left( \frac{1}{x} \right) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{\ln x}{2\sqrt{x}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \sqrt[3]{x^4} \ln x = x^{\frac{4}{3}} \ln x \\ f'(x) &= \left( \frac{4}{3} x^{\frac{1}{3}} \right)(\ln x) + (x^{\frac{4}{3}})\left( \frac{1}{x} \right) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{4}{3} \sqrt[3]{x} \ln x + \sqrt[3]{x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \sqrt[3]{x^4} e^x = x^{\frac{4}{3}} e^x \\ f'(x) &= \left( \frac{4}{3} x^{\frac{1}{3}} \right)e^x + (x^{\frac{4}{3}})(e^x) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{4}{3} \sqrt[3]{x} \, e^x + \sqrt[3]{x^4} \, e^x} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \sqrt[3]{x^5} \, 6^x = x^{\frac{5}{3}} 6^x \\ f'(x) &= \left( \frac{5}{3} x^{\frac{2}{3}} \right)6^x + (x^{\frac{5}{3}})(6^x \ln 6) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{5}{3} \sqrt[3]{x^2} \, 6^x + \sqrt[3]{x^5} \, 6^x \ln 6} \end{aligned}$$
