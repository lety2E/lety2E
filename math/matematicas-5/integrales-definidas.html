Integrales definidas

Apuntes — Regla de Barrow

$$\int_{a}^{b} f(x) \, dx = F(b) - F(a)$$

La función $F$ se llama primitiva o antiderivada: su derivada es la función $f$, es decir $F' = f$.

$\displaystyle \int x^n \, dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C$

Ejemplo

$$\begin{aligned} \int_{-3}^{5} (4x^2 - x) \, dx &= \left[ \frac{4x^3}{3} - \frac{x^2}{2} \right]_{-3}^{5} \\ &= \left[ \frac{4(5)^3}{3} - \frac{(5)^2}{2} \right] - \left[ \frac{4(-3)^3}{3} - \frac{(-3)^2}{2} \right] \\ &= \left[ \frac{4(125)}{3} - \frac{25}{2} \right] - \left[ \frac{4(-27)}{3} - \frac{9}{2} \right] \\ &= \frac{500}{3} - \frac{25}{2} + \frac{108}{3} + \frac{9}{2} \\ &= \frac{608}{3} - \frac{16}{2} = \frac{1168}{6} = \mathbf{\frac{584}{3}} \approx \mathbf{194.67} \end{aligned}$$

Respuestas

▼
Bloque 1
Ejercicio 1
$$\begin{aligned} \int_{-2}^{4} (5x^2 + 6x) \, dx &= \left[ \frac{5x^3}{3} + 3x^2 \right]_{-2}^{4} \\ &= \left[ \frac{5(4)^3}{3} + 3(4)^2 \right] - \left[ \frac{5(-2)^3}{3} + 3(-2)^2 \right] \\ &= \left[ \frac{5(64)}{3} + 3(16) \right] - \left[ \frac{5(-8)}{3} + 3(4) \right] \\ &= \left[ \frac{320}{3} + 48 \right] - \left[ \frac{-40}{3} + 12 \right] \\ &= \frac{320}{3} + 48 + \frac{40}{3} - 12 \\ &= \frac{360}{3} + 36 = 120 + 36 = \mathbf{156} \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 2
$$\begin{aligned} \int_{1}^{5} (4 - 5x^2) \, dx &= \left[ 4x - \frac{5x^3}{3} \right]_{1}^{5} \\ &= \left[ 4(5) - \frac{5(5)^3}{3} \right] - \left[ 4(1) - \frac{5(1)^3}{3} \right] \\ &= \left[ 20 - \frac{5(125)}{3} \right] - \left[ 4 - \frac{5(1)}{3} \right] \\ &= 20 - \frac{625}{3} - 4 + \frac{5}{3} \\ &= 16 - \frac{620}{3} \\ &= \frac{48 - 620}{3} = \mathbf{-\frac{572}{3}} \approx \mathbf{-190.67} \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 3
$$\begin{aligned} \int_{-2}^{3} (-4x^2 + x) \, dx &= \left[ -\frac{4x^3}{3} + \frac{x^2}{2} \right]_{-2}^{3} \\ &= \left[ -\frac{4(3)^3}{3} + \frac{(3)^2}{2} \right] - \left[ -\frac{4(-2)^3}{3} + \frac{(-2)^2}{2} \right] \\ &= \left[ -\frac{4(27)}{3} + \frac{9}{2} \right] - \left[ \frac{32}{3} + \frac{4}{2} \right] \\ &= -\frac{108}{3} + \frac{9}{2} - \frac{32}{3} - 2 \\ &= -\frac{140}{3} + \frac{5}{2} \\ &= \frac{-280 + 15}{6} = \mathbf{-\frac{265}{6}} \approx \mathbf{-44.17} \end{aligned}$$
Bloque 2
Ejercicio 4
$$\begin{aligned} \int_{3}^{4} (-2x^2 - 7) \, dx &= \left[ -\frac{2x^3}{3} - 7x \right]_{3}^{4} \\ &= \left[ -\frac{2(4)^3}{3} - 7(4) \right] - \left[ -\frac{2(3)^3}{3} - 7(3) \right] \\ &= \left[ -\frac{128}{3} - 28 \right] - \left[ -\frac{54}{3} - 21 \right] \\ &= -\frac{128}{3} - 28 + \frac{54}{3} + 21 \\ &= -\frac{74}{3} - 7 \\ &= \frac{-74 - 21}{3} = \mathbf{-\frac{95}{3}} \approx \mathbf{-31.67} \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 5
$$\begin{aligned} \int_{-1}^{5} (-8 + 3x) \, dx &= \left[ -8x + \frac{3x^2}{2} \right]_{-1}^{5} \\ &= \left[ -8(5) + \frac{3(5)^2}{2} \right] - \left[ -8(-1) + \frac{3(-1)^2}{2} \right] \\ &= \left[ -40 + \frac{75}{2} \right] - \left[ 8 + \frac{3}{2} \right] \\ &= -40 + \frac{75}{2} - 8 - \frac{3}{2} \\ &= -48 + \frac{72}{2} = -48 + 36 = \mathbf{-12} \end{aligned}$$
Ejercicio 6
$$\begin{aligned} \int_{4}^{6} (5x^2 - 4x) \, dx &= \left[ \frac{5x^3}{3} - 2x^2 \right]_{4}^{6} \\ &= \left[ \frac{5(6)^3}{3} - 2(6)^2 \right] - \left[ \frac{5(4)^3}{3} - 2(4)^2 \right] \\ &= \left[ \frac{1080}{3} - 72 \right] - \left[ \frac{320}{3} - 32 \right] \\ &= \frac{1080}{3} - 72 - \frac{320}{3} + 32 \\ &= \frac{760}{3} - 40 \\ &= \frac{760 - 120}{3} = \mathbf{\frac{640}{3}} \approx \mathbf{213.33} \end{aligned}$$
