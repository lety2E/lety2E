Derivada por definición

Apuntes

La derivada de una función $f(x)$ medida bajo la definición de límite se determina con la fórmula del cociente de diferencias:

$$f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$$

Para resolverla se calcula $f(x+h)$, se sustituye en el límite, se simplifica el numerador para poder factorizar y cancelar la $h$ que causa la indeterminación, y al final se evalúa el límite.

Ejemplos

Ejemplo 1

Dada $f(x) = 5x - 3$, con $f(x+h) = 5(x+h) - 3$:

$$\begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{5(x+h) - 3 - (5x - 3)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{5x + 5h - 3 - 5x + 3}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{5h}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} 5 \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{5} \end{aligned}$$
Ejemplo 2

Dada $f(x) = 2x^2 + 4$, con $f(x+h) = 2(x+h)^2 + 4$:

$$\begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{2(x+h)^2 + 4 - (2x^2 + 4)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{2(x^2 + 2xh + h^2) + 4 - 2x^2 - 4}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{2x^2 + 4xh + 2h^2 - 2x^2}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{h(4x + 2h)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} (4x + 2h) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{4x} \end{aligned}$$

Respuestas

▼
Bloque 1
Dada $f(x) = 4x + 2$
$$\begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{4(x+h) + 2 - (4x + 2)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{4x + 4h + 2 - 4x - 2}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{4h}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} 4 \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{4} \end{aligned}$$
Dada $f(x) = 6x - 5$
$$\begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{6(x+h) - 5 - (6x - 5)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{6x + 6h - 5 - 6x + 5}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{6h}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} 6 \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{6} \end{aligned}$$
Dada $f(x) = 3x^2$
$$\begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{3(x+h)^2 - 3x^2}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{3(x^2 + 2xh + h^2) - 3x^2}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{3x^2 + 6xh + 3h^2 - 3x^2}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{h(6x + 3h)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} (6x + 3h) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{6x} \end{aligned}$$
Bloque 2
Dada $f(x) = -5x^2$
$$\begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{-5(x+h)^2 - (-5x^2)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{-5(x^2 + 2xh + h^2) + 5x^2}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{-5x^2 - 10xh - 5h^2 + 5x^2}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{h(-10x - 5h)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} (-10x - 5h) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{-10x} \end{aligned}$$
Dada $f(x) = x^2 - 3x - 2$
$$\begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{(x+h)^2 - 3(x+h) - 2 - (x^2 - 3x - 2)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{x^2 + 2xh + h^2 - 3x - 3h - 2 - x^2 + 3x + 2}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{2xh + h^2 - 3h}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{h(2x + h - 3)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} (2x + h - 3) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{2x - 3} \end{aligned}$$
Dada $f(x) = x^2 - x + 5$
$$\begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{(x+h)^2 - (x+h) + 5 - (x^2 - x + 5)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{x^2 + 2xh + h^2 - x - h + 5 - x^2 + x - 5}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{2xh + h^2 - h}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{h(2x + h - 1)}{h} \\ &= \lim_{h \to 0} (2x + h - 1) \\ \mathbf{f'(x)} &= \mathbf{2x - 1} \end{aligned}$$
