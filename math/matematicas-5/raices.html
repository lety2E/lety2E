Matemáticas 5 · Tema 4

Derivadas de raíces

Apuntes

Para derivar una raíz, primero la transformamos en un exponente fraccionario usando la regla:

$$\sqrt[n]{x^m} = x^{\frac{m}{n}}$$

Ejemplos

$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= -6\sqrt[3]{x^4} \\ f(x) &= -6x^{\frac{4}{3}} \\ f'(x) &= -6\left(\frac{4}{3}\right)x^{\frac{4}{3} - \frac{3}{3}} \\ &= \left(-\frac{6}{1}\right)\left(\frac{4}{3}\right)x^{\frac{1}{3}} \\ &= -\frac{24}{3}x^{\frac{1}{3}} \\ &= \mathbf{-8\sqrt[3]{x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} g(x) &= 5\sqrt[4]{x} \\ g(x) &= 5x^{\frac{1}{4}} \\ g'(x) &= (5)\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)x^{\frac{1}{4} - \frac{4}{4}} \\ &= \frac{5}{1}\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)x^{-\frac{3}{4}} \\ &= \frac{5}{4}x^{-\frac{3}{4}} \\ &= \frac{5}{4x^{\frac{3}{4}}} \\ &= \mathbf{\frac{5}{4\sqrt[4]{x^3}}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} h(x) &= \frac{-5}{6\sqrt[3]{x^2}} \\ h(x) &= \left(-\frac{5}{6}\right)x^{-\frac{2}{3}} \\ h'(x) &= \left(-\frac{5}{6}\right)\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)x^{-\frac{2}{3} - \frac{3}{3}} \\ &= \frac{10}{18}x^{-\frac{5}{3}} \\ &= \frac{10}{18x^{\frac{5}{3}}} \\ &= \mathbf{\frac{5}{9\sqrt[3]{x^5}}} \end{aligned}$$

Ejercicios

Bloque 1
$f(x) = -4\sqrt{x^5}$
$f(x) = -5\sqrt{x^3}$
$f(x) = 7\sqrt[4]{x}$
$f(x) = -9\sqrt[5]{x^2}$
$f(x) = \dfrac{-2}{6\sqrt[3]{x^4}}$
$f(x) = \dfrac{-6}{4\sqrt[3]{x^2}}$
Bloque 2
$f(x) = 8\sqrt[3]{x^2}$
$f(x) = -2\sqrt[5]{x^3}$
$f(x) = 6\sqrt{x}$
$f(x) = -4\sqrt[4]{x^5}$
$f(x) = \dfrac{-3}{9\sqrt{x^3}}$
$f(x) = \dfrac{-10}{5\sqrt[4]{x}}$

Respuestas

▼
Bloque 1
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= -4\sqrt{x^5} \\ f(x) &= -4x^{\frac{5}{2}} \\ f'(x) &= -4\left(\frac{5}{2}\right)x^{\frac{5}{2} - \frac{2}{2}} \\ &= -\frac{20}{2}x^{\frac{3}{2}} \\ &= \mathbf{-10\sqrt{x^3}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= -5\sqrt{x^3} \\ f(x) &= -5x^{\frac{3}{2}} \\ f'(x) &= -5\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)x^{\frac{3}{2} - \frac{2}{2}} \\ &= \mathbf{-\frac{15}{2}\sqrt{x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= 7\sqrt[4]{x} \\ f(x) &= 7x^{\frac{1}{4}} \\ f'(x) &= 7\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)x^{\frac{1}{4} - \frac{4}{4}} \\ &= \frac{7}{4}x^{-\frac{3}{4}} \\ &= \mathbf{\frac{7}{4\sqrt[4]{x^3}}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= -9\sqrt[5]{x^2} \\ f(x) &= -9x^{\frac{2}{5}} \\ f'(x) &= -9\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)x^{\frac{2}{5} - \frac{5}{5}} \\ &= -\frac{18}{5}x^{-\frac{3}{5}} \\ &= \mathbf{-\frac{18}{5\sqrt[5]{x^3}}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{-2}{6\sqrt[3]{x^4}} \\ f(x) &= -\frac{1}{3}x^{-\frac{4}{3}} \\ f'(x) &= \left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)\left(-\frac{4}{3}\right)x^{-\frac{4}{3} - \frac{3}{3}} \\ &= \frac{4}{9}x^{-\frac{7}{3}} \\ &= \mathbf{\frac{4}{9\sqrt[3]{x^7}}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{-6}{4\sqrt[3]{x^2}} \\ f(x) &= -\frac{3}{2}x^{-\frac{2}{3}} \\ f'(x) &= \left(-\frac{3}{2}\right)\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)x^{-\frac{2}{3} - \frac{3}{3}} \\ &= 1x^{-\frac{5}{3}} \\ &= \mathbf{\frac{1}{\sqrt[3]{x^5}}} \end{aligned}$$
Bloque 2
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= 8\sqrt[3]{x^2} \\ f(x) &= 8x^{\frac{2}{3}} \\ f'(x) &= 8\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)x^{\frac{2}{3} - \frac{3}{3}} \\ &= \frac{16}{3}x^{-\frac{1}{3}} \\ &= \mathbf{\frac{16}{3\sqrt[3]{x}}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= -2\sqrt[5]{x^3} \\ f(x) &= -2x^{\frac{3}{5}} \\ f'(x) &= -2\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)x^{\frac{3}{5} - \frac{5}{5}} \\ &= -\frac{6}{5}x^{-\frac{2}{5}} \\ &= \mathbf{-\frac{6}{5\sqrt[5]{x^2}}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= 6\sqrt{x} \\ f(x) &= 6x^{\frac{1}{2}} \\ f'(x) &= 6\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)x^{\frac{1}{2} - \frac{2}{2}} \\ &= 3x^{-\frac{1}{2}} \\ &= \mathbf{\frac{3}{\sqrt{x}}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= -4\sqrt[4]{x^5} \\ f(x) &= -4x^{\frac{5}{4}} \\ f'(x) &= -4\left(\frac{5}{4}\right)x^{\frac{5}{4} - \frac{4}{4}} \\ &= -5x^{\frac{1}{4}} \\ &= \mathbf{-5\sqrt[4]{x}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{-3}{9\sqrt{x^3}} \\ f(x) &= -\frac{1}{3}x^{-\frac{3}{2}} \\ f'(x) &= \left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)\left(-\frac{3}{2}\right)x^{-\frac{3}{2} - \frac{2}{2}} \\ &= \frac{1}{2}x^{-\frac{5}{2}} \\ &= \mathbf{\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x^5}}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= \frac{-10}{5\sqrt[4]{x}} \\ f(x) &= -2x^{-\frac{1}{4}} \\ f'(x) &= (-2)\left(-\frac{1}{4}\right)x^{-\frac{1}{4} - \frac{4}{4}} \\ &= \frac{1}{2}x^{-\frac{5}{4}} \\ &= \mathbf{\frac{1}{2\sqrt[4]{x^5}}} \end{aligned}$$
