Regla de la cadena (P1)

Apuntes

La regla de la cadena se utiliza para derivar funciones compuestas:

$$h(x) = f(g(x)) \longrightarrow h'(x) = [f'(g(x))][g'(x)]$$

Ejemplos

$$\begin{aligned} h(x) &= -2(4 - 5x^2)^3 \\ h'(x) &= -6(4 - 5x^2)^2(-10x) \\ h'(x) &= 60x[16 - 40x^2 + 25x^4] \\ \mathbf{h'(x)} &= \mathbf{960x - 2400x^3 + 1500x^5} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} g(x) &= 3(2x^3 + 5x^2)^2 \\ g'(x) &= 6(2x^3 + 5x^2)^1(6x^2 + 10x) \\ g'(x) &= 6(12x^5 + 30x^4 + 20x^4 + 50x^3) \\ \mathbf{g'(x)} &= \mathbf{72x^5 + 300x^4 + 300x^3} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} h(x) &= \sqrt[3]{4x^2 - 5} = (4x^2 - 5)^{\frac{1}{3}} \\ h'(x) &= \frac{1}{3}(4x^2 - 5)^{-\frac{2}{3}}(8x) \\ h'(x) &= \frac{8x}{3(4x^2 - 5)^{\frac{2}{3}}} \\ \mathbf{h'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{8x}{3\left(\sqrt[3]{4x^2 - 5}\right)^2}} \end{aligned}$$

Ejercicios

Bloque único
$h(x) = 8(3 - 4x^2)^3$
$g(x) = 4(5x^2 - 2)^3$
$h(x) = 4(5x - 2x^2)^2$
$g(x) = -5(2x^3 - 6x)^2$
$h(x) = \sqrt[5]{3x^2 - 4}$
$g(x) = \sqrt[3]{6 - 7x^2}$

Respuestas

▼
Bloque único
$$\begin{aligned} h(x) &= 8(3 - 4x^2)^3 \\ h'(x) &= 24(3 - 4x^2)^2(-8x) \\ h'(x) &= -192x(9 - 24x^2 + 16x^4) \\ \mathbf{h'(x)} &= \mathbf{-1728x + 4608x^3 - 3072x^5} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} g(x) &= 4(5x^2 - 2)^3 \\ g'(x) &= 12(5x^2 - 2)^2(10x) \\ g'(x) &= 120x(25x^4 - 20x^2 + 4) \\ \mathbf{g'(x)} &= \mathbf{3000x^5 - 2400x^3 + 480x} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} h(x) &= 4(5x - 2x^2)^2 \\ h'(x) &= 8(5x - 2x^2)^1(5 - 4x) \\ h'(x) &= 8(25x - 20x^2 - 10x^2 + 8x^3) \\ \mathbf{h'(x)} &= \mathbf{64x^3 - 240x^2 + 200x} \end{aligned}$$
Bloque único (sigue)
$$\begin{aligned} g(x) &= -5(2x^3 - 6x)^2 \\ g'(x) &= -10(2x^3 - 6x)^1(6x^2 - 6) \\ g'(x) &= -10(12x^5 - 12x^3 - 36x^3 + 36x) \\ \mathbf{g'(x)} &= \mathbf{-120x^5 + 480x^3 - 360x} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} h(x) &= \sqrt[5]{3x^2 - 4} = (3x^2 - 4)^{\frac{1}{5}} \\ h'(x) &= \frac{1}{5}(3x^2 - 4)^{-\frac{4}{5}}(6x) \\ \mathbf{h'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{6x}{5\left(\sqrt[5]{3x^2 - 4}\right)^4}} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} g(x) &= \sqrt[3]{6 - 7x^2} = (6 - 7x^2)^{\frac{1}{3}} \\ g'(x) &= \frac{1}{3}(6 - 7x^2)^{-\frac{2}{3}}(-14x) \\ \mathbf{g'(x)} &= \mathbf{\frac{-14x}{3\left(\sqrt[3]{6 - 7x^2}\right)^2}} \end{aligned}$$
