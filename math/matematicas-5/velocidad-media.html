Velocidad media e instantánea

Apuntes

Velocidad media
Representa el cambio de posición en un intervalo de tiempo determinado: vm=ΔxΔt=x(t2)−x(t1)t2−t1v_m = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t} = \frac{x(t_2) - x(t_1)}{t_2 - t_1}vm​=ΔtΔx​=t2​−t1​x(t2​)−x(t1​)​
Velocidad instantánea
Es el valor de la velocidad en un punto exacto del tiempo: la derivada de la función de posición: v(t)=x′(t)=lim⁡Δt→0x(t+Δt)−x(t)Δtv(t) = x'(t) = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{x(t + \Delta t) - x(t)}{\Delta t}v(t)=x′(t)=Δt→0lim​Δtx(t+Δt)−x(t)​

Ejemplo

Una partícula se mueve según la ecuación x(t)=16t−t2x(t) = 16t - t^2x(t)=16t−t2.

a) Posición en t=0t=0t=0 y t=2t=2t=2 x(0)=16(0)−(0)2=0x(2)=16(2)−(2)2=32−4=28\begin{aligned} x(0) &= 16(0) - (0)^2 = \mathbf{0} \\ x(2) &= 16(2) - (2)^2 \\ &= 32 - 4 = \mathbf{28} \end{aligned}x(0)x(2)​=16(0)−(0)2=0=16(2)−(2)2=32−4=28​
b) Velocidad media en (0,2)(0, 2)(0,2) vm=x(2)−x(0)2−0vm=28−02=14\begin{aligned} v_m &= \frac{x(2) - x(0)}{2 - 0} \\ v_m &= \frac{28 - 0}{2} = \mathbf{14} \end{aligned}vm​vm​​=2−0x(2)−x(0)​=228−0​=14​
c) Velocidad instantánea en t=1.2t=1.2t=1.2 x′(t)=16−2tx′(1.2)=16−2(1.2)x′(1.2)=16−2.4=13.6\begin{aligned} x'(t) &= 16 - 2t \\ x'(1.2) &= 16 - 2(1.2) \\ x'(1.2) &= 16 - 2.4 = \mathbf{13.6} \end{aligned}x′(t)x′(1.2)x′(1.2)​=16−2t=16−2(1.2)=16−2.4=13.6​

Ejercicio 1
a) x(1)=(1)2−5(1)=1−5=−4x(3)=(3)2−5(3)=9−15=−6\begin{aligned} x(1) &= (1)^2 - 5(1) = 1 - 5 = \mathbf{-4} \\ x(3) &= (3)^2 - 5(3) = 9 - 15 = \mathbf{-6} \end{aligned}x(1)x(3)​=(1)2−5(1)=1−5=−4=(3)2−5(3)=9−15=−6​
b) vm=x(3)−x(1)3−1vm=−6−(−4)2vm=−22=−1\begin{aligned} v_m &= \frac{x(3) - x(1)}{3 - 1} \\ v_m &= \frac{-6 - (-4)}{2} \\ v_m &= \frac{-2}{2} = \mathbf{-1} \end{aligned}vm​vm​vm​​=3−1x(3)−x(1)​=2−6−(−4)​=2−2​=−1​
c) x′(t)=2t−5x′(1.8)=2(1.8)−5x′(1.8)=3.6−5=−1.4\begin{aligned} x'(t) &= 2t - 5 \\ x'(1.8) &= 2(1.8) - 5 \\ x'(1.8) &= 3.6 - 5 = \mathbf{-1.4} \end{aligned}x′(t)x′(1.8)x′(1.8)​=2t−5=2(1.8)−5=3.6−5=−1.4​
Ejercicio 2
a) x(2)=(2)2+4(2)=4+8=12x(4)=(4)2+4(4)=16+16=32\begin{aligned} x(2) &= (2)^2 + 4(2) = 4 + 8 = \mathbf{12} \\ x(4) &= (4)^2 + 4(4) = 16 + 16 = \mathbf{32} \end{aligned}x(2)x(4)​=(2)2+4(2)=4+8=12=(4)2+4(4)=16+16=32​
b) vm=x(4)−x(2)4−2vm=32−122vm=202=10\begin{aligned} v_m &= \frac{x(4) - x(2)}{4 - 2} \\ v_m &= \frac{32 - 12}{2} \\ v_m &= \frac{20}{2} = \mathbf{10} \end{aligned}vm​vm​vm​​=4−2x(4)−x(2)​=232−12​=220​=10​
c) x′(t)=2t+4x′(2.5)=2(2.5)+4x′(2.5)=5+4=9\begin{aligned} x'(t) &= 2t + 4 \\ x'(2.5) &= 2(2.5) + 4 \\ x'(2.5) &= 5 + 4 = \mathbf{9} \end{aligned}x′(t)x′(2.5)x′(2.5)​=2t+4=2(2.5)+4=5+4=9​
Ejercicio 3

b) vm=x(5)−x(1)5−1vm=−47−14vm=−484=−12\begin{aligned} v_m &= \frac{x(5) - x(1)}{5 - 1} \\ v_m &= \frac{-47 - 1}{4} \\ v_m &= \frac{-48}{4} = \mathbf{-12} \end{aligned}vm​vm​vm​​=5−1x(5)−x(1)​=4−47−1​=4−48​=−12​
c) x′(t)=−4tx′(3)=−4(3)=−12\begin{aligned} x'(t) &= -4t \\ x'(3) &= -4(3) = \mathbf{-12} \end{aligned}x′(t)x′(3)​=−4t=−4(3)=−12​
